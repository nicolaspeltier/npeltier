Research Interests

I am mainly interested in automated reasoning, (computational) logic and related topics (rewriting, logic programming etc.).
  • Separation Logic
  • Superposition calculus
  • Inductive theorem proving
  • Formalization of proofs in Isabelle
  • Tableaux calculus

I am the scientific leader of the CAPP group.
